CRIPTechnical Architecture
SIH 2026 · Problem Statement 26023 · Ministry of Coal

1 · End-to-end pipeline

From a scanned coal report to an approved, bilingual Parliament reply. Numbers come from the Golden Record by SQL and code; narrative comes from the Vector Index; the offline LLM only phrases the answer, which is checked and then approved by an officer before export.

data flow flagged for a human data store design guarantee

2 · System Architecture

Everything runs inside the government network boundary. The LLM runs locally; no component calls an outside AI service.

3 · Request Lifecycle

One Parliamentary question, step by step — routed to facts or narrative, drafted by the local LLM, checked, approved by an officer, and remembered.

Properties of the architecture itself, not bolt-on policy.

Role-based access

Five officer roles, each scoped to its own work.

Full provenance
